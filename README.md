# Gomitai tasks

Gomitai's public project repo: the shared record of work on Gomitai, a TOPC venture about cleaning, started by Intesar. Each TOPC project has its own tasks repo and ledger.

- [Issues](https://github.com/topc-social/gomitai-tasks/issues): open tasks. Each issue is one task, and its `neki:N` label is the Neki it earns (1 Neki = 1 rupee of work at market price).
- [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md): who has earned what. Your share of Gomitai = your Neki / all Neki there.

## How it works

1. Pick any open issue. Anyone can hand in, and the best hand-in wins.
2. Do the work however you like, including with your own AI.
3. Hand in with a pull request (or through Ideato, which opens one for you) that says `Closes topc-social/gomitai-tasks#N`.
4. Merging the hand-in accepts it: the issue gets `accepted` and a workflow adds the two Neki rows (1% idea, 99% work) to [`LEDGER.md`](LEDGER.md).

## Rules

- Proof must be something others can open, and anyone you quote or record has to agree to it.
- Made-up people, answers or numbers earn nothing.
- Neki is a record of your work and becomes your share in what gets built. More: https://www.ideato.social/neki
